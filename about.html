How we evaluate a story

  • Evidence Strength How closely the accumulated evidence, based on tier source data, supports the claim — Low, Moderate, High, or Collapsed.
  • Narrative Direction Which way the story is currently trending — Rising, Falling, or Steady.
  • Clusters The number of independent lines of evidence behind the current Evidence Strength — not just repeated mentions of the same source.
  • Tier How directly a source impacts the claim. Tier 1 is a primary source — a court filing, a direct statement, a government record. Tier 2 is reporting or secondary analysis. Tier 3 is commentary, opinion, or unverified reports — social posts, punditry, anything not yet independently confirmed — weighted lowest of the three.

How we resolve a claim

  • Confirmed The claim held up as stated.
  • Disproven The claim did not hold up.
  • Partial Part of the claim held. Part of it didn't.

*If new evidence on a topic surfaces, we reopen the claim and the status returns to Ongoing, the prior outcome is cleared, and the evidence trajectory picks up where it left off.
