Two Kinds of Signals

  • Long-running trends Patterns that may take years to fully resolve, if they ever do. Premise Strength updates as new evidence arrives, without forcing a premature conclusion.
  • Bounded claims Specific, dated commitments — a price, a date, a charge — that resolve in weeks or months. These get scored once the outcome is known.

Both are tracked the same way: evidence accumulates, Premise Strength updates, and nothing is treated as resolved until it actually is.

How We Score a Signal

  • Premise Strength How closely the accumulated evidence still lines up with the premise a signal started with — Low, Moderate, High, or Collapsed. This isn't a belief we're asserting: it's computed directly from each piece of evidence's tier and direction, plotted against when that evidence actually came out. We're not telling you how much to believe something — we're reporting how the evidence has moved.
  • Velocity Which direction Premise Strength is currently moving — Rising, Falling, or Steady.
  • Clusters The number of independent lines of evidence behind the current Premise Strength — not just repeated mentions of the same source.
  • Tier How directly a piece of evidence bears on the claim. Tier 1 is a primary source — a filing, a direct statement, a government record. Tier 2 is reporting or secondary analysis. Tier 3 is commentary, opinion, or unverified reports — social posts, punditry, anything not yet independently confirmed — weighted lowest of the three.

How a Claim Resolves

  • Hit The claim held up as stated.
  • Missed The claim did not hold up.
  • Partial Part of the claim held. Part of it didn't.

Every bounded claim gets one of these, in public, whether or not anyone is still paying attention by the time it resolves.

What This Isn't

  • Not a prediction market We don't set odds or take positions on outcomes we can't yet see.
  • Not advice We don't tell you what to buy, vote for, or believe.
  • Not breaking news If something happened five minutes ago, this probably isn't the place for it yet.
