Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Wake (wake.news), operated by WEP Ventures LLC, a Georgia limited liability company ("WEP Ventures," "we," "us," or "our"), collects when you visit this website (the "Site"), and how we handle it. We built the Site to be a simple, static publication, and we try to collect no more than that requires.

1. Information We Collect

The Site itself doesn't require an account, and doesn't ask you to submit personal information to read it. We do receive standard technical information automatically whenever you visit — things like IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and referring URL — collected through our hosting provider's server logs and any basic analytics we enable. If you email us directly, we'll have whatever information you choose to include in that email.

2. How We Use Information

We use the technical information described above to keep the Site running, understand overall traffic patterns, and diagnose problems. We use anything you send us directly (such as an email) to respond to you. We don't use this information to build advertising profiles, and we don't sell it.

3. Cookies and Similar Technology

The Site may use minimal, functional cookies (for example, to remember a filter selection) and privacy-conscious analytics provided by our hosting platform. We don't use third-party advertising trackers. If that changes, we'll update this policy.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only with the service providers that host and run the Site on our behalf (for example, our hosting and database providers), and only as needed for them to provide that service to us. We don't sell personal information, and we only disclose it beyond that if required to by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of WEP Ventures LLC or others.

5. Data Retention

We keep technical/server-log information only as long as reasonably useful for the purposes described above, and any email correspondence for as long as needed to address your inquiry and maintain our own business records.

6. Your Choices

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. You can also email us at any time to ask what information we hold about you or to request that we delete correspondence you've sent us, and we'll do our best to honor that request consistent with our legal obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests regarding your information, can be sent to info@wake.news.
